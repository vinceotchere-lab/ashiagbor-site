## 3. About page content

### Full biography

```text
Dr George Ashiagbor is a geospatial scientist, Senior Lecturer, and Head of the Department of Wildlife and Range Management at the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Kumasi, Ghana. He has served as Head of Department since January 2025 and as Senior Lecturer since August 2022.

His research addresses land-use change in forest–agriculture landscapes. It brings together four connected areas: mapping and independent validation of forest and land-cover products for regulatory compliance; socio-ecological effects of cash-crop and mining expansion; habitat and biodiversity assessment; and precision agronomy and canopy modelling in cocoa agroforestry systems.

Dr Ashiagbor applies GIS, satellite remote sensing, spatial modelling, machine learning, field validation, and quantitative methods to develop practical evidence for conservation, climate action, traceable commodity supply chains, natural-resource governance, and sustainable rural livelihoods.

Alongside research, he has a substantial academic leadership record. He coordinates the MPhil/MSc Geo-Information Science for Natural Resources Management (GISNATUREM) programme, has led departmental accreditation and curriculum redevelopment work, and has supported the transition to online learning and assessment at Faculty, College, and University levels. He has supervised postgraduate and undergraduate research across forest monitoring, carbon stocks, cocoa systems, watershed processes, wildlife habitat, and environmental change.
```

### Research themes
